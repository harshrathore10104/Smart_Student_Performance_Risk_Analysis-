# Project Statement

## Problem Statement
Students and academic staff often need to combine marks, attendance, subject performance, and risk indicators to understand academic progress. Manual analysis can be repetitive and makes it difficult to identify students who may need early academic support.

## Scope
The system provides student record management, academic analytics, visualization, machine-learning-based risk classification, and PDF report generation.

## Target Users
- Students
- Faculty members
- Academic coordinators

## High-Level Features
1. Student CRUD management
2. Performance analytics
3. Attendance analysis
4. Academic risk prediction
5. Interactive dashboard
6. PDF report generation
7. Automated validation and testing
